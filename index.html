AI-Powered Job & Company Insight Agentic System :

A smart job-seeker tool that combines Kafka-based event-driven architecture with AI automation to deliver real company insights. The system starts by scraping LinkedIn job listings, which are streamed via Kafka to trigger a full AI workflow in n8n. It analyzes each company's website, section by section, using AI models to extract meaningful insights. The output is a clean, structured Markdown report, organized by job title — helping users go beyond job descriptions and understand the companies they're applying to.
